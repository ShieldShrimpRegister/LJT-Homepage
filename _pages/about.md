I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am a member of the **HKUST NLP Group** and work under the supervision of **Professor Junxian He**, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning.

## Biography

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree, and I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Computer Science at HKUST, where I continue to work with Professor Junxian He. Before starting my doctoral studies, I spent time on internship experiences at three research groups: MINIMAX, Tencent WXG and Shanghai AI Lab. Along the way I have worked on LLM reasoning and data synthesis, vision-language model perception, and machine learning methods for improving the trustworthiness of large language models.
